From a distributor feed

To stock a part that's only in a distributor feed: copy the listing as a new in-shop product (its own SKU, stock from 0; the listing itself is never changed), or link it to a part you already have. A vendor whose feed prefix matches the listing gets the listing's SKU as its part number.

Listings

Your active GunBroker listings, matched to in-shop products by SKU. A linked fixed-price listing's quantity follows WooCommerce stock (less the buffer). Feed products and guns are never linked; auctions aren't synced. To link a listing, give it the product's SKU on GunBroker.

Sales brought in

Each GunBroker sale of a linked product becomes a WooCommerce order (paid, processing, with the buyer's shipping address but no email, so WooCommerce sends them nothing), and stock goes down once. Returns work by scanning the WooCommerce order number.

Label design

What the Zebra prints (drawn from its own instructions)

The QR code holds the SKU. The SKU prints as large as the label allows, on two lines when that makes it bigger. The preview shows the first product on the list.

"Read wide" uses the same 2 × 3 in labels as upright: they still feed through the Zebra 2 in across, and the label is printed sideways, like the Locator's gun tags.

On the Zebra, the text uses the printer's own font, which is a little narrower than the preview's.

Each person scans their badge (USR- and their code) before scanning products, so every change is logged under their name. The Wojtek Locator's badges work here too: import its People export once. To sign out, scan CMD-LOGOUT or wait 10 idle minutes.

Station terminals

A terminal is a station computer or tablet with its own key, so it never needs the shop password. Open the setup link once on that device. Revoking it stops it at once. Terminals can scan and change stock, but can't change Setup or settings.

Distributor feed prefixes

Products whose SKU starts with a ticked prefix are never shown at the station and never changed. These groups come from the SKUs in your store, largest first: tick every distributor feed. A prefix covers everything that starts with it, so IRV also covers IRV-123 and IRVX9.

Serialized gun categories

Products in a ticked category, and their variations, are never changed here: their stock moves through FastBound and sales.

Possible double restocks

Returns Wojtek Stock already put back in stock, then refunded in WooCommerce with "Restock refunded items" ticked, so WooCommerce added them again. Check each product's stock and lower it with Adjust (Count correction) if it's now too high.

Stock changed outside sales

Stock that changed without a WooCommerce order or a Wojtek Stock change to explain it: an edit in wp-admin, another plugin or app (such as BuckFFL or a marketplace sync), or a register that doesn't create orders. Worth a look if these show up often.
